One flat monthly fee WiFi for the whole home No cable to dig

Across much of Niger, home internet is either unavailable or unaffordable.

Many families depend on mobile data, which is costly and capped. That makes everyday things — schoolwork, running a business, staying in touch — harder than they need to be.

Zumfi exists to make dependable home connectivity affordable for ordinary households, closing the digital gap for communities that current networks do not reach.

  • Predictable cost. A flat monthly fee, so the bill doesn't rise every time the children have homework.
  • Built for a household. One connection covers the whole family, on every device, at the same time.
  • Reaches where cable doesn't. The connection arrives by air, so no street has to be dug up to serve your home.
  • A proven model. The same technical approach already operates successfully in other international markets.

How the service works

Three parts, all of them standard equipment used by wireless internet operators worldwide.

1

A connection at the tower

We equip a fixed tower site with a high-capacity internet connection — the backbone link to the wider internet — and wireless transmission equipment.

2

Broadcast across the area

Tower-mounted sector antennas send that connection across a fixed coverage area, reaching the homes within range of the site.

3

WiFi inside your home

A small receiving dish on your roof picks up the signal and delivers WiFi indoors, for a flat monthly fee. Installation is handled by our team.

Why it stays affordable: a single tower serves many households at once, so the cost per home stays low. That is what makes the service work for the mass market rather than a privileged few.

Technical overview

Backbone
A high-capacity internet connection at each tower site, providing the link to the wider internet.
Distribution
Tower-mounted sector antennas that broadcast the connection across a fixed coverage area.
Customer equipment
A small rooftop receiver at each subscriber home, delivering WiFi indoors.
