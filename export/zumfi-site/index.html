How it works

Three parts — all of it standard equipment, used by wireless internet operators worldwide.

Connected at the tower

We bring a high-capacity internet connection to a tower site, with the equipment to send it out.

Sent across the area

Antennas on the tower broadcast it across the neighbourhood, to every home in range.

WiFi in your home

A small dish on your roof picks it up and gives you WiFi indoors. We handle the install.

Zumfi is securing the licences and authorizations required to operate in Niger, and every piece of equipment will be type-approved and lawfully imported before it goes up.
