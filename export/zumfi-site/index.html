Our mission

Internet should open doors, not create barriers.

For many families and communities in Niger, dependable home internet can still be slow, unreliable, or too expensive. Zumfi is being built to help change that.

Our mission is to make reliable home connectivity more accessible and affordable, giving more people the opportunity to learn, work, communicate, build businesses, and stay connected.

Focused on everyday households. Beginning with the needs of communities that have limited dependable connectivity options.
